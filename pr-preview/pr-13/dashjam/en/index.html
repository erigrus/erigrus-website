Never miss a jam.

DashJam records your rehearsal continuously and lets you save the last few minutes — after something good happens. No more “wait, were we even recording?”

Download on the App Store

Free, with an optional DashJam Pro in-app upgrade. iPhone, iOS 18 or later. English and German.

How it works

  • Start session — DashJam starts listening right away.
  • Keep playing. The app holds the most recent 15 minutes in a rolling buffer.
  • When something good happens: Save Jam — drag the handles on the live waveform to keep exactly the stretch that mattered.
  • Saved jams land in your Library and as .m4a files in the Files app.

What the app does

  • Record continuously, decide later. You choose the moment after it happens, not before.
  • Save from the Lock Screen. A Live Activity and Dynamic Island show the recording state and put a save action a tap away.
  • It survives a crash. If the app gets killed mid-session, your rolling buffer is still there when you reopen.
  • A library you'll actually browse. Auto-generated names like “Crimson Spiral”, renameable, with in-app playback.
  • Your files, your way. Export as .m4a via AirDrop, Messages, or any share sheet.
  • English and German, with an in-app language override.

DashJam Pro

Everything above is free. DashJam Pro is an optional in-app upgrade that adds:

  • a rolling buffer of up to 60 minutes instead of 15,
  • automatic jam detection (Beta),
  • trimming saved jams from head and tail — keeping the original or replacing it,
  • Places: geofenced spots like your rehearsal room, with reminders when you arrive or leave,
  • the audio quality and mono controls.

Available as a yearly or monthly subscription, or as a one-time Lifetime purchase — owning any one of them unlocks Pro. Prices are shown in your own currency in the app under Settings → Get DashJam Pro, before you buy.

Privacy-first

Audio never leaves your device — no servers, no accounts, no tracking. You decide whether and when to share a recording. The Places you save for Pro, and the coordinates behind them, stay on your device too and are never transmitted.
