How DashJam works

  • Tap Start session — DashJam begins listening right away.
  • Keep playing. The app holds the most recent 15 minutes of audio in a rolling buffer.
  • When something good happens, tap Save Jam and drag the handles on the live waveform to pick exactly the stretch you want, then confirm.
  • Saved jams land in your Library, where you can play them back, rename them, and export them. They also appear in the iOS Files app under DashJam.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. There's no sign-in. DashJam works entirely on your device.

Where do my recordings go?

Onto your iPhone, and nowhere else. Saved jams are .m4a files in DashJam's folder in the Files app. Nothing is uploaded.

Why does DashJam need the microphone?

To record your session. Audio stays on your device.

Does it record in the background?

Yes — while a session is active, recording continues when the app is in the background, so you don't have to keep it on screen. It stops when you end the session.

How far back can I save?

As far back as your rolling buffer reaches: 15 minutes for everyone, and up to 60 minutes with DashJam Pro, where the buffer length is adjustable in Settings.

My session was recording but I closed the app — did I lose it?

The rolling buffer is crash-safe and is restored when you reopen the app, as long as iOS hasn't evicted the cache to reclaim storage.

How do I get my recording off the phone?

Open the jam in your Library (or find it in the Files app under DashJam) and use the share sheet — AirDrop, Messages, email, or any destination you like.

Which languages are supported?

English and German. You can override the language in Settings → Language.

DashJam Pro

DashJam is free, and everything above works without paying: continuous recording, a 15-minute buffer, saving on the waveform, the Library, playback, and export. DashJam Pro is an optional upgrade that adds:

  • a rolling buffer of up to 60 minutes instead of 15,
  • automatic jam detection (Beta) in the save flow,
  • trimming saved jams from head and tail on the waveform, saving the result as a copy or replacing the original,
  • Places: geofenced spots like your rehearsal room, with reminders when you arrive or leave,
  • and the audio quality and mono controls.

How do I buy it?

Settings → Get DashJam Pro. The card lists everything Pro unlocks and the three ways to buy it: a yearly subscription, a monthly subscription, or a one-time Lifetime purchase. Prices are shown there in your own currency before you buy. Owning any one of the three unlocks Pro.

I bought Pro but the app doesn't show it.

Open Settings → Get DashJam Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you're signed in to the same Apple Account you bought with. If the card says purchases aren't available right now, that's usually a connection problem — tap Try Again.

How do I cancel my subscription?

In your Apple Account settings: Settings app → your name → Subscriptions → DashJam. Cancelling there stops future renewals; you keep Pro until the current period ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends.

I bought Lifetime but I'm still subscribed.

Then you're paying for something you already own. DashJam will point this out and offer a Manage Subscription button — cancelling the subscription is safe, Pro stays unlocked.

Do I need to buy Pro again on my other iPhone?

No. Purchases follow your Apple Account. Sign in with the same account and tap Restore Purchases.

Places and location

Places is the only part of DashJam that uses your location, and it only asks when you switch a Place reminder on. It watches a circle around a spot you saved so it can remind you to start or save a recording there — which needs background location, because the reminder has to reach you while the app is closed. The places and coordinates stay on your device and are never transmitted. If you don't use Places, DashJam never asks for your location.
